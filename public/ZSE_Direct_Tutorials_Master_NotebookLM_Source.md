# SECTION 1: CAPITAL MARKETS ECOSYSTEM & REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 The Zimbabwe Stock Exchange (ZSE)
Established in 1894, the Zimbabwe Stock Exchange is the premier licensed stock exchange in Zimbabwe, operating under the regulatory supervision of the **Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe (SECZim)** in terms of the Securities and Exchange Act [Chapter 24:25]. Headquartered at **44 Ridgeway North, Highlands, Harare**, ZSE provides a primary market for corporate capital raising and a secondary market for equity and debt trading.

### 1.2 Market Participants & Their Roles (Authentic ZSE Framework)
1. **The Investor**: The supplier of capital who buys shares (equity) to earn dividend income and achieve long-term capital appreciation.
2. **Securities Dealers (Licensed Stockbrokers)**: The registered member firms authorized to transact on the ZSE. By law, retail investors cannot trade in a vacuum; every ZSE Direct user must be sponsored and mapped to a licensed stockbroker (e.g., *Imara Edwards Securities, Old Mutual Securities, FBC Securities, MMC Capital, Morgan & Co, Lynton-Edwards Securities, Invictus Securities, EFE Securities*). Stockbrokers execute, provide advisory services, and handle client account custody.
3. **Central Securities Depository (CSD - Chengetedzai Depository Company / ZSE Depository)**: An electronic custody registry that dematerializes physical paper share certificates into electronic book-entry records. The CSD holds uncertificated shares and coordinates settlement.
4. **Securities Custodians**: Financial institutions responsible for safe-keeping of investor assets, settlement of transactions, and collection of dividends. Retail accounts can be broker-controlled (custodied by the stockbroker) or held with independent custody institutions.
5. **Transfer Secretaries**: Organizations (such as Corpserve or First Transfer Secretaries) appointed by listed companies to manage the master register of shareholders, disburse dividend payments, and administer corporate actions.
6. **Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe (SECZim)**: The statutory market regulator responsible for investor protection, market surveillance, licensing market intermediaries, and enforcing Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards.
7. **Investor Protection Fund (IPF)**: Statutory safety fund established under the Securities and Exchange Act to compensate retail investors against losses arising from negligence, insolvency, or malfeasance by registered market intermediaries.

### 1.3 Trading Sessions & Automated Trading System (ATS)
The ZSE operates on an electronic high-frequency Automated Trading System (ATS). Trading follows a strict Central Africa Time (CAT) daily schedule:

| Trading Phase | Schedule (CAT) | Permitted Activities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Open Session** | 09:00 AM – 09:30 AM | Order entry, price modification, and order cancellation. No trades match. ATS calculates the Indicative Equilibrium Price (IEP). |
| **Opening Auction** | 09:30 AM | Single-price matching algorithm uncrosses the order book, executing all overlapping bids and asks at equilibrium price. |
| **Continuous Trading** | 09:30 AM – 13:00 PM | Real-time continuous order matching based strictly on **Price-Time Priority (FIFO - First In, First Out)**. |
| **Market Close** | 13:00 PM | Order entry and execution cease. Official Volume-Weighted Average Prices (VWAP) and daily closing indices are calculated. |
| **Settlement Cycle** | **T+3 Rolling Settlement** | Cleared transfer of electronic shares at the CSD against cleared cash funds takes place 3 business days after trade execution ($T+3$). |

# SECTION 2: ZSE DIRECT RETAIL PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 What is ZSE Direct?
Launched in 2020 by ZSE Holdings, **ZSE Direct** (`https://zsedirect.co.zw`) is an official retail digital portal designed to dismantle historical participation barriers. It connects retail Zimbabwean citizens directly into the ZSE Automated Trading System (ATS) via modern web browsers and native mobile applications (Android & iOS).

Through ZSE Direct, retail investors independently:
- View live real-time stock price movements, corporate news announcements, and market indices (ZSE All Share, Top 10, Medium Cap).
- Access electronic Level 2 Market Depth (inspecting active buyer bids and seller asks).
- Submit electronic Buy Orders and Sell Orders straight into the exchange order book.
- Monitor active personal portfolio valuation, dividend payouts, and cash ledger balances.

# SECTION 4: STEP-BY-STEP TRADING WALKTHROUGHS

## 4.1 Wallet Top-Up & Funding Mechanisms
To place an order, cleared funds must be available in your ZSE Direct trading wallet:
- **Instant Gateways**:
  - **Paynow / EcoCash / InnBucks / Zimswitch**: Instant wallet credit upon mobile PIN authorization.
- **Direct Bank Transfers (ZIPIT & RTGS)**:
  - Transferred to the designated ZSE Direct Custody Trust Account.
  - **Crucial Requirement**: You **must** quote your unique **ZSE Direct Account Number or National ID** in the bank payment transfer reference field. If the reference is omitted, automated matching cannot credit your wallet and manual POP verification is required.

## 4.2 Order Book Anatomy & Placing Buy Orders
- **The Order Book**:
  - **Bids (Buy Orders)**: Buyers enter the maximum price in ZiG they are willing to pay and the quantity of shares demanded.
  - **Asks / Offers (Sell Orders)**: Sellers enter the minimum price in ZiG they are willing to accept and the quantity of shares supplied.
  - **The Spread**: The gap between the Highest Bid and Lowest Ask.
- **Order Execution Types**:
  - **Limit Order**: You stipulate the exact maximum price per share. The ATS will execute only at that price or a better (lower) price. If no seller matches, your order sits in the central queue.
  - **Market Order**: You instruct the system to purchase immediately at the current lowest available ask price in the market.
- **Time-in-Force (TIF)**:
  - **Day Order (DAY)**: Valid only until the close of trading at 13:00 CAT today. Any unexecuted volume is cancelled.
  - **Good-Till-Cancelled (GTC)**: Remains active in the central order book day after day (up to 90 calendar days) until filled or manually cancelled.

## 4.3 Trading Fees & Statutory Levies (Buy & Sell Breakdown)
Every transaction is subject to standard statutory fees transparently previewed before order confirmation:
- **Brokerage Commission**: ~1.00% – 1.50% (Remunerates your licensed stockbroker).
- **SECZim Regulatory Levy**: 0.16% (Supports statutory market regulation).
- **CSD Levy**: 0.10% (Electronic depository custody and clearing).
- **Investor Protection Fund (IPF)**: 0.05% (Retail investor safety net).
- **Stamp Duty (ZIMRA)**: 0.25% (Applicable on buy transactions).
- **Capital Gains Withholding Tax (CGWT)**: Withheld at source by ZIMRA on sales of shares according to holding duration.
- *Total transaction friction averages approximately 1.7% to 2.5% of gross trade consideration.*

## 4.4 Selling Shares & Fund Withdrawals
- **Portfolio Dematerialization Verification**: You can only sell shares verified and held in your electronic CSD account.
- **Placing Sell Orders**: Select the counter from your active portfolio, enter quantity, specify limit price, and submit.
- **Withdrawals**: Once shares are sold and settle on the $T+3$ schedule, cleared cash reflects in your "Withdrawable Balance". Withdrawals can be sent only to your verified bank account on file following OTP security verification.
